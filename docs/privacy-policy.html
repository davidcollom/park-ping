Park Ping privacy policy

Effective date: 8 October 2026

Park Ping is an independent Android app developed by David Collom (GitHub: davidcollom). This policy describes the current app, version 0.1.0.

Location

If you grant location permission and start Park mode, Android provides your approximate or precise device location to Park Ping. The app uses it on your device to calculate straight-line distances between you and rides and to decide whether your alert rules match. Your coordinates are not sent to Park Ping's developer or to ThemeParks.wiki, and the app does not save them to its preferences or feed cache.

The latest location fix is held temporarily in app memory during the session and cleared when the session ends or the service is destroyed. Android and the location provider configured on your device may process location under their own privacy terms.

Network requests and third parties

The app requests public ride and queue data from ThemeParks.wiki over HTTPS. Requests identify the selected park and use the ThemeParks.wiki public API. As with any internet request, the service can see the connecting IP address and ordinary request metadata. Park Ping does not operate a server and does not receive these requests. The service's own logging, use and retention are governed by its policies and practices, which Park Ping does not control.

The app does not include an account, advertising, analytics, or crash-reporting service. It does not send your ride alert rules, favourites, notification history, or location to the developer or the data provider.

Information stored on your device

  • App settings store your selected park, favourite ride IDs, alert rules, ride-date markers, and notification cooldown timestamps in Android app-private preferences.
  • Ride and queue responses are cached in the app's private cache for offline display. Park metadata is refreshed when older than 24 hours; live data is replaced after a successful refresh. Cache files can otherwise remain until replaced or cleared.
  • These settings and cached feeds are not included in Android backup. They remain on the device until you clear Park Ping's app data/cache or uninstall the app; alert settings can also be removed in the app.

Park Ping does not keep a copy of this information on a developer-operated server. Android may remove cache files at any time.

Park mode and permissions

Park mode starts only after you tap its start control, review the explanation, and grant the needed location and notification permissions. It uses an Android location foreground service, with an ongoing notification and a Stop Park mode action. You can stop it in the app or from that notification. A session ends after 12 hours; it is not automatically restarted after the app or service is terminated.

Location permission is needed for nearby alerts, and notification access is needed to start Park mode. You can deny either permission and continue browsing ride data. You can change or revoke permissions in Android settings; if location or notifications are unavailable, Park mode stops or cannot start. Android battery controls may delay checks.

Your choices and contact

You can remove alert rules in the app, change location and notification permissions in Android settings, and erase locally stored app data by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling Park Ping. There is no account or server-held profile to delete.

For support or privacy questions, contact the developer through Park Ping's GitHub issue tracker. Please do not include precise location, passwords, or other sensitive information in a public issue.

Changes

This policy will be updated if the app's data practices change. The current version is published in the Park Ping repository.
