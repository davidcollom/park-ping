# Park Ping mascot and identity

An original cheerful location-pin park scout, holding a ticket and waving. Teal/mint, golden accents and navy outlines. The transparent PNG at [images/park-ping-mascot.png](images/park-ping-mascot.png) remains the character artwork master. Android app icons, Play artwork and their editable SVG layouts are in [brand/](brand/) and [store-assets/](store-assets/).

The Android launcher uses a teal adaptive-icon background and a safely inset mascot foreground, with a simplified monochrome location-pin layer for themed icons. Notifications use a separate solid-white bell glyph; Android tints it as needed. App screens use the mascot with dark navy text and teal accents.

Play exports target a 512 × 512 PNG app icon and a 1024 × 500 opaque feature graphic. Both have editable SVG layout sources; the mascot itself is linked from the transparent PNG master. These are candidate assets based on the supplied concept. Dave's review has not been recorded, so approve the identity before publishing.

## Generation brief

Use case: logo-brand. Asset type: Park Ping app mascot avatar for Android theme park queue alerts and GitHub README. Create one original friendly mascot: a rounded location-pin shaped little park scout, large warm expressive eyes and an easy smile, tiny waving arm, a small ticket-like accent. Clean polished flat illustration with subtle depth, bold simple silhouette that works at app icon sizes. Use teal and mint with a warm golden accent, dark navy outlines. Centered full character with generous safe margin, no words, no lettering, no brand logos, no Disney or Universal characters, no scenery. Transparent background.
